Privacy Policy

Nothing leaves your Mac.

Last updated: October 15, 2026

In short: Netoff does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server. The app contains no code that sends data anywhere. Everything Netoff records stays on your Mac, and you can delete it at any time.

What Netoff looks at

To do its job — letting you allow or block internet access app by app — Netoff's network extension is told by macOS about every network connection that a program on your Mac opens. For each connection it handles:

  • the name and location of the program that opened it;
  • the destination domain name or IP address, the port and the protocol (TCP or UDP);
  • whether the connection was allowed or blocked, and why;
  • how many bytes were sent and received, to show speeds.

Netoff does not read, record or modify the contents of your traffic — not web pages, messages, passwords or files. The one exception is plain (unencrypted) DNS answers: Netoff reads these in memory, only to show a domain name next to an IP address. They are not saved.

Sharing and third parties

Netoff doesn't share data with anyone, because it doesn't send data anywhere. It has no third-party software development kits, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting of its own.

If you get Netoff from the Mac App Store, Apple may collect information about downloads and, if you've allowed it in your system settings, usage and crash data shared with developers. That is governed by Apple's privacy policy. Netoff does not receive personal data from it.

Permissions Netoff asks for

  • Network extension (content filter). Required. This is how macOS lets Netoff see and control connections. You approve it in System Settings.
  • Notifications. Optional. Used for a single heads-up when a blocked app keeps trying to connect. You can turn it off in Netoff's Settings.
  • Open at login. Optional. Off unless you turn it on.

Deleting your data

  • Connection log: Privacy Report › Connection Log › Clear Log. Or set it to “Don’t Keep a Log”.
  • Privacy report: Privacy Report › Clear Report.
  • Activity list: Activity › Clear, or quit Netoff.
  • Rules: Settings › Reset All Rules… (or edit or delete rules.json)
  • Everything: do the above, then choose Settings › Uninstall Extension… and move Netoff to the Trash.

Children

Netoff doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date. Because Netoff collects nothing, we don't expect that to change what the app does with your data.

Contact
